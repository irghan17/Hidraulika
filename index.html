📐 Formula Kunci

Manning (yn):

Q = (1/n)·A·R^(2/3)·S₀^½
A=(b+m·y)·y P=b+2y√(1+m²) R=A/P

Kedalaman Kritis (yc):

g·A³/T = Q² (Fr = 1)
T = b + 2m·y

Energi & Gaya Spesifik:

Es = y + Q²/(2g·A²)
Fs = Q²/(g·A) + b·y²/2 + m·y³/3

Loncatan Hidrolik:

Fs(y₁) = Fs(y₂) → y₂ konjugat
ΔE = Es(y₁) − Es(y₂) [kehilangan energi]
L_j ≈ 6·(y₂−y₁) [Peterka empiris]

Standard Step (profil):

Sf = (n·V/R^(2/3))²
ΔX = (E₂−E₁) / (S₀ − S̄f)
